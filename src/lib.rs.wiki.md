# src/lib.rs

**Role:** Crate root for the std-only Wikifier core.

Public modules are `api`, `artifacts`, `cache`, `jsonutil`, `maps_out`, `mcp`, `mcp_groups` (MCP tool groups and their switches), `mcp_compact` (compact default MCP replies), `parse`, `scope`, `sha256`, `symbols`, `text_out`, and `timeutil`. `legacy_cache`, `project_lock`, `cache_commit`, `cycle_report`, `daemon_svc`, `low_conf`, `pending_line`, `wiki_files`, and `proc` stay private. Re-exported names: `cache_status`, `check_changes`, `health`, `list_paths`, `load_dependencies`, `load_dependents`, `mapped_edges`, `mark_green`, `prepare_edit`, `read_health`, `read_journal`, `record_change`, `session_bootstrap`, `suggest_next_actions`, `update_maps`, `VERSION`, and `Json`.
